# Changelog

## v0.17.0 — 2026-11-16

- Tride 0.17.0 writes format 0.77 (0.16.x wrote 0.76). It keeps a scene's environments as a list (each one lights, is shown, or both), adds a material type, Shadow catcher, and draws an Unlit material through a compiled material on primitives, clones and models with an emission colour. Every scene Tride 0.17.0 saves, including a project from 0.16 or earlier saved again, needs Tride 0.17.0 or later on every player: Tride 0.16.x refuses it with the "newer format" error that asks to update Tride. Scenes saved by earlier Tride open in 0.17.0 with the look they had; one whose Unlit material now needs a compiled material reports that it was saved by an older version of Tride until it is exported again.
- A scene's environment is now a list, top first: each environment is a colour, a bundled preset or your own picture, and lights the scene, is shown behind it, or both, with its own opacity, intensity and rotation. The top environment that lights the scene lights it; the shown ones are laid over each other, and where nothing is shown the background is transparent. Scenes saved by earlier Tride open in 0.17.0 with the look they had.
- A node material made of glass keeps its look over a transparent background, and a Shadow catcher on a primitive, a clone or a model from a file receives the shadow without casting one of its own.
- A new material type, Shadow catcher (Material window, Type): the surface shows only the shadow the sun casts on it, so an object can stand on the scene's background with its shadow and no floor in sight. Base Color is the shadow's colour and Opacity its strength; choosing the type sets black at 70 %. It shows the shadow of a directional light only: spot and point lights, the environment's light and ambient occlusion give none, and the editor says so when no directional light casts shadows. Another glTF viewer shows nothing of such a surface, and players older than this release cannot open a file that has one.
- An Unlit material now looks the same on every object: flat, with no light or shade, and its Emission glows. Before, Unlit on a primitive or a clone was drawn lit, and an Emission colour on an Unlit model from a file did not show; a scene exported before keeps its old look until it is exported again. A glTF material marked unlit imports with Emission off, as other glTF viewers show it, and the import message says so.
- `advance` refuses a script step with a field it does not know and names the field and the step, for example `step 1: unknown field pointr`. Before, such a field was ignored and the step ran without it.
- Switching the render knobs with `setRenderOverrides` (MSAA, bloom, shadows, depth of field) no longer rebuilds the colour grading table when the tone mapping did not change, so the call holds the main thread several milliseconds less. A change of tone mapper, exposure, luminance scaling, gamut mapping or saturation still rebuilds it.
- A scene without its own camera now shows small models: the default camera's clipping planes follow the scene size, so a model of a few centimetres or millimetres is no longer cut away and the frame is no longer empty. Scenes of a metre and larger look as before.
- A scene with a transparent background makes `TrideSceneView` non-opaque by itself, so the SwiftUI views under it show through; an opaque scene keeps an opaque view. The README lists what a transparent background changes. In TrideCore, `backgroundColor()` is gone: `environmentFrame()` gives what the scene's environments light and show.
